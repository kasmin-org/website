---
title: About the name
description: Where the name Kasmin comes from.
---

Kasmin stands for **K**ids' **S**ummer camp **Admin**.

The name comes from the [Kindersommerlager Wiental](https://ks-wiental.at) ("KS Wiental"), a volunteer-run summer
camp for children from Vienna, Austria. KS is short for *Kindersommerlager*, German for children's summer camp. We
built Kasmin for our own camp because we wanted a tool in which families manage their children themselves and the
routine work runs on its own. Now it is free and open source, so every other camp can use it as well.

Kasmin is maintained by the [Kindersommerlager Wiental](https://ks-wiental.at).
