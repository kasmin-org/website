<Aside type="caution" title="Early development">
Kasmin is in early development (versions 0.x) and not ready for production use yet. Version 1.0 will follow after
thorough testing.
</Aside>

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Family accounts" icon="seti:folder">
		Legal guardians manage their children and their data themselves. Siblings belong to one family; emergency
		contacts are entered once for the whole family.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Login without passwords" icon="approve-check">
		One-time login links and codes by e-mail. Administrative roles add a second factor.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Registrations and waitlist" icon="list-format">
		Places and age limits per role, automatic waitlist with visible position, reserve places for special cases.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Safety sheet" icon="warning">
		Fully configurable, confirmed once a year with one click instead of filled in again, encrypted and visible only
		to a few people.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Automation and API" icon="setting">
		Invoices with sibling discounts, mailings and reminders that run by themselves – with previews and approval.
		Everything also via REST API and MCP for AI assistants.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Runs anywhere" icon="laptop">
		PHP and MariaDB/MySQL on ordinary web hosting (one-file installer) or as a Docker image. Free software under
		the AGPL-3.0.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
